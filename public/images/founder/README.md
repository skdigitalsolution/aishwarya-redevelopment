# Founder & Executive Leadership Assets

This directory contains executive photography and credentials for the Founder / Managing Director of Aishwarya Redevelopment Advisory Company.

## Image Requirements

### 1. Executive Editorial Portrait (`founder-portrait.jpg` / `.webp`)
- **Aesthetic**: Refined architectural/corporate editorial portraiture (consistent with luxury advisory branding like Primality).
- **Background**: Deep warm charcoal, subtle architectural navy, or warm ivory studio setting.
- **Aspect Ratio**: 4:5 or 3:4 portrait orientation.
- **Recommended Dimensions**: 1200x1500px or 1600x2000px at 72dpi sRGB.
- **Lighting**: Soft directional key light, sophisticated professional attire.

### 2. Digital Signature (`founder-signature.svg` / `.png`)
- **Format**: Clean transparent vector `.svg` or high-resolution `.png`.
- **Color**: Muted gold (`#C6A66B`) or deep navy (`#0B1D3A`).
- **Use Case**: Closing fiduciary pledge, letter from the founder, advisory endorsement.
