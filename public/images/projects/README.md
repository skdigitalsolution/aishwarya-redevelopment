# Redevelopment Projects & Architectural Visuals

This directory stores project imagery, architectural elevations, before/after redevelopment transformations, and 3D architectural renders.

## Image Requirements

### 1. Project Showcase Photography (`*.webp`, `*.jpg`)
- **Dimensions**: 1920x1080px (16:9) or 1600x1000px (16:10)
- **Quality**: Optimized WebP (< 300KB each)
- **Subject**: Architectural facades, master plan aerial views, premium lobby renders, site context.

### 2. Before / After Comparison Assets
- Matched perspective angles of existing cooperative housing societies and future proposed redeveloped towers.
