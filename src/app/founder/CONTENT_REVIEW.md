AISHWARYA REDEVELOPMENT ADVISORY COMPANY

LEADERSHIP PROFILE & FOUNDER INTRODUCTION



“Led by Vision. Built on Trust. Driven by Uncompromising Results.”— Executive Profile: Mr. Sachin Murudkar, Founder & Director



1. Executive Summary & Leadership Profile

In the dynamic and highly nuanced landscape of Mumbai real estate, structural redevelopment represents far more than mere architectural transformation. It is the complex convergence of legacy preservation, legal precision, urban economics, and human trust. At the forefront of this critical industry stands Mr. Sachin Murudkar, Founder and Director of Aishwarya Redevelopment Advisory Company.

Operating out of Mumbai—a micro-market where every square foot carries immense financial and emotional weight—Mr. Murudkar has earned an unshakeable reputation as one of the region's premier redevelopment strategists, land valuation experts, and corporate advisors.

Over the past decade of continuous on-ground excellence, Mr. Murudkar has transitioned from an ambitious real estate strategist into an authoritative figure across Western Mumbai. His consultancy serves housing societies, commercial plot owners, and legal entities seeking absolute transparency, structural compliance, and maximum financial realization.

Rather than treating redevelopment as a standard commercial deal, Mr. Murudkar views each project through a holistic lens. He balances municipal regulatory frameworks, urban planning laws, and developer financial health with the deeply personal requirements of society residents and landowning families.

Under his executive vision, Aishwarya Redevelopment Advisory Company has evolved into a full-service strategic advisory firm. The company bridges the historical gap between cooperative housing societies and real estate developers through rigorous due diligence and sophisticated project modeling.

His leadership philosophy is anchored in long-term value creation. By refusing to compromise on regulatory compliance, legal clarity, and transparent feasibility reporting, Mr. Murudkar has consistently shielded clients from predatory market practices and operational delays.

2. A Decade of Excellence: Institutionalizing Trust in Mumbai  & Extended Corridors

What originated ten years ago as a specialized advisory desk in Mumbai , has scaled systematically into a market-leading advisory institution. Over this decade, Mr. Sachin Murudkar has meticulously mapped and mastered the real estate dynamics of key Western Suburb pockets

Understanding Mumbai's urban sprawl requires more than statutory knowledge; it demands granular familiarity with neighborhood histories, community needs, and micro-market land titles. Mr. Murudkar's decade-long immersion across these territories provides him with unrivaled insights into local development parameters and land administration.

Throughout his 10-year career, Mr. Murudkar has successfully guided over 500 CTS plots through complex urban planning, entitlement, and development workflows. His portfolio includes celebrated interventions in intricate City Survey divisions, such as CTS 146, CTS 156, and dense Gaothan parcels.

In the domain of collective housing, Mr. Murudkar has served as the principal advisor to over 100 old cooperative housing societies undergoing traditional developer-driven redevelopment as well as pioneer-led Self-Redevelopment models.

His advisory firm has executed more than 200 comprehensive Title Search and legal due diligence investigations. These technical audits trace property ownership from City Survey offices to the Inspector General of Registration (IGR), eliminating title risks prior to project commencement.

Furthermore, Mr. Murudkar has structured and facilitated dozens of high-value Joint Venture (JV) and Joint Development Agreements (JDA). Through strategic negotiation and optimized Floor Space Index (FSI) calculations, he has consistently secured up to 40% higher financial valuation and area allocations for landowners compared to prevalent market benchmarks.

His technical command shines brightest when resolving complex structural and legal cases. Notable examples include managing old load-bearing properties such as Sindhumati House and Bhanu Niwas configurations located within narrow, access-constrained Gaothan lanes.

3. Core Advisory Competencies & Strategic Pillars

Mr. Sachin Murudkar’s distinct position in Mumbai’s real estate advisory landscape is built upon five foundational pillars of strategic expertise:

I. Mastery over Municipal & Administrative Procedures

Navigating Mumbai’s administrative landscape requires complete fluency in municipal protocols. Mr. Murudkar maintains deep knowledge of administrative procedures across Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC) wards—including R/North Ward—City Survey Offices, and District Collectorate divisions. From securing Intimation of Disapproval (IOD) and Commencement Certificates (CC) to executing Property Registration (PR) Card corrections, Section 11 Deemed Conveyance procedures, and obtaining final Occupation Certificates (OC), his end-to-end statutory mastery turns administrative obstacles into seamless approvals.

II. Unmatched Title Resolution & Due Diligence

Real estate projects in Mumbai frequently falter due to clouded titles, historical encumbrances, and fragmented ownership records. Mr. Murudkar is widely recognized as a premier specialist in complex land titles. Whether resolving ambiguous 7/12 extracts, disputed PR Cards, or multi-owner CTS subdivisions (such as CTS 156/1 and 156/2), his forensic title audits provide crystal-clear legal status. Legal practitioners, real estate developers, and financial institutions regularly solicit his technical counsel.

III. The Premier Redevelopment Strategist of Mumbai

The aging residential infrastructure of  Greater Mumbai  demands precise developer selection and robust financial structuring. Mr. Murudkar provides comprehensive Feasibility Reports that evaluate developer solvency, track records, financial liquidity, and technical capabilities. His advisory ensures that housing societies secure maximum usable carpet area, guaranteed timely rental compensation, and bulletproof bank-backed corpus funds.

IV. Pioneer & Champion of Self-Redevelopment

Recognizing the growing friction in traditional builder-led models, Mr. Murudkar emerged as an early advocate of Self-Redevelopment in Borivali West. Under his guidance, housing societies transition from passive property owners into autonomous project leads, capturing 100% of the project's profit margin. He provides end-to-end handholding—from securing institutional finance and appointing reputable Project Management Consultants (PMCs) to contractor selection and site monitoring.

V. Innovative Land Amalgamation & Value Maximization

Small, isolated plots (such as standard 107.2 Sq.Mtr / 1,153 Sq.Ft parcels) often struggle to unlock viable development potential under prevailing DCPR regulations. Mr. Murudkar pioneered specialized land amalgamation strategies, combining contiguous micro-plots into unified, high-FSI buildable parcels. This strategic aggregation dramatically increases plot valuation and turns constrained properties into lucrative corporate development sites.
